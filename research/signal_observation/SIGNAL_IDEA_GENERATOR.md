# Signal Idea Generator

## Purpose

This is a lightweight source of raw research ideas.

It is not evidence, not validation, not an active setup stage, and not a
replacement for backlog, EXPLORE, triage, hypothesis notes, or formal
validation.

## Mechanism Questions

- Who is forced to trade regardless of price?
- Who pays for leverage or convenience?
- Where is there predictable forced behavior?
- Where is there structural information asymmetry?

## When To Use

Use conditionally:

- backlog falls below 2 triage-ready-or-higher candidates;
- an active setup is parked;
- a formal research cycle closes and the next candidate is unclear.

## Throughput Orientation

- Orientation target: 3-5 raw/plausible ideas in the idea pool.
- Orientation target: 2-3 triage-ready-or-higher candidates in backlog.
- Never add a weak idea merely to satisfy these numbers.

### Liquidation Cascades

- Mechanism: forced liquidation flow may create short-lived directional continuation or overshoot.
- Potential payer: overleveraged traders forced out and liquidity takers absorbing one-sided flow.
- Data: liquidation history or proxies, OHLCV, possibly open interest later.
- Distinctness: forced deleveraging rather than price-trend continuation.
- Explore question: do liquidation clusters show crude forward response or overshoot worth formalizing?
- Status: promoted

### Basis / Cash-and-Carry Dislocation

- Mechanism: spot-derivative basis dislocations may reflect leverage demand, financing stress, or slow arbitrage.
- Potential payer: leveraged directional traders or participants slow to arbitrage dislocations.
- Data: spot prices, perpetual/futures prices, derived basis or spread series, OHLCV.
- Distinctness: cross-market carry / financing pressure, not funding-only or price-only trend.
- Explore question: do large basis dislocations show crude mean-reversion or persistence worth formalizing?
- Status: promoted

### Options Expiry / Dealer Hedging Pressure

- Mechanism: concentrated expiry positioning may create predictable hedging or pinning pressure.
- Potential payer: dealers, market makers, or participants positioned into expiry-related flows.
- Data: options expiry calendar, public positioning/open-interest summaries if available, underlying OHLCV.
- Distinctness: options / hedging-flow family distinct from TSMOM, funding, and liquidation flow.
- Explore question: do expiry windows show crude underlying response or pinning behavior worth formalizing?
- Status: promoted
