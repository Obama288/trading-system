# Red Review — 2026-06-13

Status: HISTORY. An adversarial review of the whole campaign, recorded as
raised questions, NOT as adopted decisions. Strategic response deferred until
after Setup I resolves (owner decision). Captured so the challenges are not
lost and can be answered honestly rather than forgotten.

Context: after six parked/failed families (A,B,C, funding, E, H) and a seventh
(I) in feasibility, the chat assistant ran an adversarial pass against its own
strategic framing at the owner's request. The instruction was to attack the
frame, look wider, and try to break it — not to check correctness.

## The six attacks

1. The strategy-class map is itself a trap. It is a trading-textbook taxonomy
   imposed on crypto; the largest crypto edges (MEV, airdrop farming, new-
   listing dynamics, DeFi anomalies) live outside it. The map may legitimize a
   narrow view in a different textbook rather than widen it.

2. The whole project premise may be wrong. Retail algotrading on free public
   data via public APIs may be structurally losing against colocated, paid-
   data, staffed, capitalized firms — and the number of attempts changes
   nothing. If there is no honest answer to "where is our advantage over these
   players", every column of the map dies the same way.

3. The pipeline dictates what we can see. simcore measures signal-stop-target
   strategies; it silently excludes market-making, arbitrage-hold, and yield-
   harvesting (continuous PnL, inventory, no stop/target). Choosing the "next
   class" subconsciously favors what the machine can measure — optimizing for
   measurability, not profitability. Lamppost search.

4. The multiplicity hardening may have made any realistic PASS impossible. A
   strict Bonferroni adjustment after 7+ attempts can set the bar so high that
   a real-but-modest +0.1R edge cannot clear it — while large edges don't exist
   on liquid majors. Risk: a system that mathematically cannot say "yes" to
   anything realistic, i.e. strictness as self-sabotage. (Mitigation: calibrate
   the adjustment, don't make it draconian — recorded in v1.4 as a style, not a
   guillotine.)

5. Why directional at all? Sustained retail crypto earnings often come from
   PROVIDING A SERVICE the market pays for (liquidity provision, funding-
   harvesting, staking, delta-neutral carry) — not from out-guessing direction.
   Seven attempts all tried to win a prediction game against professionals
   without once asking whether to sell the market a service instead.

6. Meta: the assistant may be optimizing to "look disciplined" rather than to
   "make money". A beautiful methodology that rejects everything is easy to
   demonstrate; profit is not. Worth asking: if in six months there is a perfect
   pipeline and zero working strategies, is that methodology success or an
   expensive way to do nothing?

## Assessment (assistant's own, post-switch)

Attacks 2, 3, 5 are the serious ones and reinforce each other: we may have lost
seven times not because of the wrong directional signal, but because we are
playing directional — a guessing game against professionals — when an honest
retail edge may lie in selling the market a service (liquidity, carry) that the
pipeline cannot even measure. Attack 4 is real but fixable by calibration.
Attack 6 is uncomfortable and unfalsifiable but worth holding.

## The open question (decision deferred to post-Setup-I)

Is directional trading on free public data structurally unwinnable for us, and
should the project pivot toward carry / liquidity-provision / event-driven
classes that the current pipeline cannot yet measure — accepting that this means
building new measurement, not just a new detector?

This is NOT decided. Setup I (the last directional, already in flight) will be
read as the final directional datapoint. After it, the owner decides between:
- continue directional (only if Setup I shows life), or
- pivot to a different strategy column (map: #4 funding-harvest, #5 event-driven
  most promising), accepting new measurement work, or
- stop active search and harden what exists (Move D).

## What was adopted now (vs deferred)

Adopted: the strategy-class map (reference), the red-reviewer role (working
practice), v1.4 multiplicity hardening + pass-is-not-proof, the data-purchase
checklist. These institutionalize the QUESTIONS so they keep getting asked.
Deferred: the directional-vs-service pivot decision itself.
